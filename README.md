# Corporate Finance (AI Plus One)

## About the book

An expanded rough draft (version 0.3) for MBA learners who already know basic financial statements and present value. A fictional distributor must decide what to invest in, how to pay for it, and how much cash to keep. You will value projects, test the cash calendar, and defend a board recommendation that states which evidence would change it.

## How to teach or study it

Work the cooling-bay decision first. The first four chapters separate value, cash and risk; later chapters add financing and competing claims before asking you to reconcile an entire board packet. Calculate before reading the worked answer. Use [three independent review problems](problems/independent-review.md) to defend your assumptions and recommendation. Code checks the numbers, not whether your recommendation makes sense. There is no separate syllabus or instructor guide in this repo.
