# Corporate Finance (AI Plus One): chapter guide

[Return to the book overview](../README.md).

### Part 1 · Find the decision

1. [A Good Investment the Firm Cannot Yet Make](../chapters/01-price-the-decision.md): Rank two investments by net present value, then test the cash needed to fund either one.
2. [Forecast the Bank Balance, Not the Month](../chapters/02-dated-liquidity.md): Reconcile dated cash movements and identify the first breach of a reserve.
3. [The Rate Is a Claim About Risk](../chapters/03-price-project-risk.md): Separate project risk embedded in cash estimates from the return required by investors.
4. [Buy Information Before Buying Equipment](../chapters/04-price-flexibility.md): Price the option to wait and make sure the contract actually preserves the choice.

### Part 2 · Decide who pays, who gets paid, and when

5. [Finance an Investment Without Disguising the Cost](../chapters/05-finance-the-investment.md): Separate an asset’s value from the timing and cost of the debt used to buy it.
6. [Return Money Without Returning the Safety Margin](../chapters/06-preserve-the-reserve.md): Test a proposed distribution against a minimum cash balance in a downside case.
7. [Buy a Business Without Paying for Your Own Work](../chapters/07-price-the-acquisition.md): Bridge standalone value, net synergies and the highest defensible acquisition price.

### Part 3 · Keep the decision defensible

8. [Hedge the Cash Exposure You Can Name](../chapters/08-hedge-a-named-exposure.md): Calculate the residual exposure when the hedge and purchase quantities diverge.
9. [Write the Rule Before Seeing the Answer](../chapters/09-precommit-the-rule.md): Write an inclusion rule and a decision trigger before looking at outcomes.
10. [Resolve the Board's Choices Together](../chapters/10-resolve-the-board-choices.md): Choose an investment, distribution and liquidity remedy on one cash timeline.

### Part 4 · Advanced finance laboratories

11. [Close the Model Before Forecasting It](../chapters/11-close-the-model.md): Link earnings, cash flow and the opening and closing balance sheet without a plug.
12. [Replace the Machine, Not the Budget Line](../chapters/12-replace-or-retain.md): Identify the incremental cash flows in a replacement decision.
13. [Value the Financing Separately](../chapters/13-value-the-financing-separately.md): Use adjusted present value to keep tax benefits and financing costs separate.
14. [Budget the Portfolio, Not the Queue](../chapters/14-budget-the-portfolio.md): Compare project bundles subject to cash and commissioning constraints.
15. [Value the Claim You Can Buy](../chapters/15-value-the-claim-you-can-buy.md): Bridge enterprise value to equity value and stress-test continuing value.
16. [Make the Bid Fit the Calendar](../chapters/16-make-the-bid-fit-the-calendar.md): Compare closing cash and contingent payments on value and liquidity separately.
17. [Keep Cash and Rates in One Currency](../chapters/17-keep-cash-and-rates-in-one-currency.md): Match currency, discount rates and dated conversion assumptions.
18. [Read the Covenant as a Cash Claim](../chapters/18-read-the-covenant-as-a-cash-claim.md): Calculate a leverage covenant using the loan’s eligible cash and earnings definitions.
19. [Count the Shares and the Money](../chapters/19-count-the-shares-and-the-money.md): Separate primary proceeds, secondary sales, fees and ownership dilution.
20. [Solve the Adversarial Board Packet](../chapters/20-solve-the-adversarial-board-packet.md): Resolve a board packet with conflicting models and specify what could reopen the decision.
